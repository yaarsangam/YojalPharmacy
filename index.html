Our Story

More Than a Pharmacy – A Family

Yojal Pharmacy was founded by David Ghimire in 2018, right here in our neighborhood. What started as a small corner shop has grown into a trusted community hub – because we treat every customer like our own.

We know that health can be complicated. That's why we take the time to explain your medications, answer your questions, and even call you to check in. We're not just dispensing pills – we're building a healthier, happier community.
